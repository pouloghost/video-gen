# Generative prompt guidelines
The goal is to give instructions like an Art Director. Describe "the image we want to see".
## Subject & Action
- Core Subject: Who/What. (e.g. "An Eighth Route Army soldier")
- Pose/Action: What are they doing? (e.g. "standing solemnly at attention")
- Key Feature: The most important identifying point? (e.g. "with a noticeable scar on his face")
- Feature/Props: Clothing, decoration, makeups, etc. (e.g. "pale blue shirt, dark pants just above knees, brown leather boots")
- Background: Any specific background elements? (e.g. "red walls, golden roofs, stone balustrade").
## Shot & Composition
- Shot Type: Full body portrait, Medium shot, Close-up, etc.
- Angle: Eye-level, Low-angle, High-angle, etc.
- Composition: Centered, Rule of thirds, Symmetrical, etc. Specify position of every subject. (e.g. "Symmetrical, man on left, woman on right").
## Lighting & Atmosphere
- Lighting Effect: Dramatic chiaroscuro, Soft diffused light, Golden hour, Rim light, etc.
- Atmosphere/Mood: Solemn, Epic, Tense, Joyful, etc.
- Color Temperature: "warm tones", "cool tones", "neutral", etc.
## Style & Medium
- Genre: cultural portraiture, environmental portraiture, street photography, landscape photography, fine art, documentary, fashion, etc.
- Style: "warm, vibrant, high-key, moody, cinematic, low-key, black and white, dreamy, realistic, hyperrealistic, etc.
- Medium: Digital painting, Oil painting, Photograph, etc.
## Lens & Focus
- DoF: Shallow depth of field (blurry background), Deep focus (entire figure sharp), etc.
- Bokeh Presence: creamy bokeh, smooth bokeh, busy bokeh, noticeable bokeh circles, etc.
- Boken Quality: soft background blur, dreamy background blur, etc.
- Lens Effect: Non-distorted portrait lens, Telephoto compression (compressed perspective), Wide-angle distortion (dynamic tension), etc.
## Be Correct:
Consider all factors to make the image correct on all aspects, e.g. history, characteristics, story style, plot, etc.
## Emphasize the Distinctive Feature:
You must identify the single most unique, recognizable feature (as defined in Distinctive features input) and place it prominently in the prompt. This is the primary anchor for consistency.
## Structuring the Prompt:
- Start with the broad strokes: Shot type, main subjects, core action.
- Integrate details: Weave in shot, composition, lighting, atmosphere, style, medium, lens, focus, etc. You can group them or spread them logically.
- Be specific: Instead of "good lighting", use "golden hour lighting, soft diffused". Instead of "blurry background", use "shallow depth of field, creamy bokeh".
- Use strong descriptive adjectives.
- Think about the desired *feeling* of the image and translate that into photographic terms.
